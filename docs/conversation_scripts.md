# Sample conversation scripts

## Script 1: General OPD - chest pain
Patient: I have chest pain since yesterday.
AI: I'm sorry to hear that. Can you point to exactly where the pain is?
Patient: Center of my chest.
AI: Does the pain spread anywhere, like your arm, jaw, or back?
Patient: No, it stays in one place.
AI: Is it a sharp pain, a dull ache, or a burning feeling?
Patient: Dull ache.
AI: Does anything make it worse, like exertion or lying down?
Patient: Worse when I climb stairs.
AI: Does it get better with rest?
Patient: Yes.
AI: Are you experiencing any shortness of breath, sweating, or nausea along with it?
Patient: No, none of that.
AI: Thank you, that gives me a clear picture. I have what I need for now.

## Script 2: Red flag case - chest pain with breathlessness
Patient: I have chest pain and I'm having trouble breathing.
AI: That combination needs urgent attention. I'm flagging this for immediate priority review. While we wait, can you tell me when this started?
Patient: About twenty minutes ago.
AI: Are you sweating or feeling dizzy as well?
Patient: Yes, both.
AI: Thank you, a staff member is being alerted right now to see you immediately.

## Script 3: AYUSH mode - digestive complaint
Patient: My digestion has been irregular for two months.
AI: I understand. How would you describe your digestion normally - is it regular, variable, or sluggish?
Patient: Variable, sometimes fast sometimes slow.
AI: How would you describe your body type overall - do you tend to run warm or cold, are you naturally thin or heavier built?
Patient: I run warm, and I'm thin built.
AI: What is your bowel movement pattern generally like - regular, irregular, or constipated?
Patient: Irregular.
AI: Have you noticed any specific triggers, like stress or certain foods?
Patient: Mostly stress and skipping meals.
AI: Thank you, this helps build a complete picture of your constitution and current imbalance.
